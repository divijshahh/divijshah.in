How it started

A karaoke bar, a PG, and a bad first date

I messaged you on Instagram out of nowhere. A few weeks later we were at a karaoke bar. You were having the time of your life, then very much not, and then I was the guy working out how to get a near-stranger back to her PG.

The morning after was supposed to be a mistake. I had decided that in advance. Somewhere in the middle of it, I think I already knew I was in trouble.

That was a year and a half ago. I have never been this open with anyone, and I have never felt this understood. I want the ordinary future: an apartment, coming home to you, and a lifetime of sending you reels you won’t watch.

I love you so much, babe. Tung tung.
